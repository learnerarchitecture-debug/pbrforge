NOTES FOR PERSONAL USE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. IMAGES YOU PROCESS
   Material packs produced by this software are derivative works of the source
   images you feed it. If those images came from a bought asset pack, its EULA
   still applies to the output. If they came from your own camera, the output is
   yours. Keep the `source` field in material.json populated so you can always
   answer "where did this come from?" months later.

2. AI MODEL WEIGHTS
